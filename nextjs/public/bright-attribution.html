Bright (OSM OpenMapTiles)

The official OSM OpenMapTiles style by MapTiler / OpenMapTiles contributors, heavily inspired by OpenStreetMap Carto (CC0). Adapted to the Nordsweet deck.gl renderer; code BSD-3-Clause, design CC-BY 4.0. Tiles: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles and © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Style · Layer styles · OpenMapTiles · OpenStreetMap · OpenFreeMap · CC-BY 4.0

Same renderer limitations as Fjord and Dark Matter: system font, collision-managed place names; line-following road/water labels, POIs, sprites and patterns are omitted, as are tunnels, roads under construction and footpaths. Bridges are drawn as ordinary roads. Other original colours, widths, opacity stops, filters, order and zoom limits are retained.

The vector tile schema has been developed by Klokan Technologies GmbH and
was initially modelled after the cartography of the CARTO's Positron basemap
with permission from CartoDB Inc.
The vector tile schema has been refined and improved in cooperation with
the Wikimedia Foundation and is heavily influenced by years of
Paul Norman's experience of creating maps from OpenStreetMap data.
