Dark Matter

Dark Matter GL style by MapTiler / OpenMapTiles contributors, based on CartoDB Basemaps by Stamen and Paul Norman. Adapted to the Nordsweet deck.gl renderer; code BSD-3-Clause, design CC-BY 4.0 (original design CC-BY 3.0). Tiles: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles and © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Style · Source revision · OpenMapTiles · OpenStreetMap · OpenFreeMap · CC-BY 4.0

Same renderer limitations as Fjord: system font, collision-managed place names; line-following road/water labels, sprites and wood pattern omitted. Other original colours, widths, opacity stops, filters, order and zoom limits are retained.
